been compromised.

https://groups.google.com/a/mozilla.org/g/dev-security-policy/c/0R3XLJL6FuE/m/0XofdebrAAAJ?pli=1

certificates in Debian accordingly.

Quick fix for critical systems:
Run
# dpkg-reconfigure ca-certicicates
and unselect all emCert certificates.

Hi,

I wanted to clear up some confusion that caused me some panic.

In this bug Stephan Verbücheln mentions four certs as affected, but I 
think that is too broad.

According to report, the ones affected are:

emSign_ECC_Root_CA_-_G3.crt  aka "CN=emSign ECC Root CA - G3"
Comodo_AAA_Services_root.crt aka "CN=AAA Certificate Services"

These other ones are not affected at this time:

emSign_ECC_Root_CA_-_C3.crt aka "CN=emSign ECC Root CA - C3"
emSign_Root_CA_-_C1.crt     aka "CN=emSign Root CA - C1"
emSign_Root_CA_-_G1.crt     aka "CN=emSign Root CA - G1"


The patches that Salvatore mentions are correct and only include the two 
affected ones and the others will not be removed at this time.

Thanks,
